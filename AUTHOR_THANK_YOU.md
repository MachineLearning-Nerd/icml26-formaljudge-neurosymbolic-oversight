# Thank You to the Authors

Thank you to Jiayi Zhou, Yang Sheng, Hantao Lou, Yaodong Yang, and Jie Fu for sharing FormalJudge: A Neuro-Symbolic Paradigm for Agentic Oversight, its paper source, and the linked implementation.

The paper presents a clear separation between probabilistic atomic semantic extraction and deterministic logical composition. That separation makes it possible to state a small local composition boundary honestly while keeping the much larger model, benchmark, and formal-runtime claims explicitly open.

This note expresses appreciation for the paper and source availability. It does not imply author review, endorsement, or agreement with the scoped audit result.
